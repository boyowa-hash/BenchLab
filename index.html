The Gap

A £30 billion industry with no way to find its own people.

The UK beauty, personal care, fragrance, and home fragrance industries form one of the fastest-growing sectors in the economy. Yet there is no platform — anywhere — where the skilled practitioners who manufacture these products can be found, verified, or hired based on what they can actually do.

🔍

The Wrong Tools

Job boards can’t distinguish a cosmetic formulator from a food scientist. LinkedIn treats “Production Operative” as one role — it’s actually hundreds of different skill profiles.

⏳

Months of Searching

Specialist production and formulation roles take 8–16 weeks to fill. Agencies charge £4,000–£10,000 per placement and still send mismatched candidates.

👻

Invisible Talent

Thousands of experienced practitioners — chandlers, soapmakers, fragrance compounders, QC technicians — are simply not findable on any existing platform. They find work through word of mouth. That’s not a system.

2

The Future of Skilled Work

The economy is moving from job titles to skills portfolios. BenchLab is already there.

AI is reshaping work away from fixed roles and toward a skills-based economy, where people market capabilities rather than rely on a single title or career ladder. For the physical industries BenchLab serves, that future is an advantage — because the skills on a production floor cannot be automated away.

01

Skills, not titles

The future of hiring is built on what people can actually do — not what their job was called. The Craftsperson’s Logbook is a skills portfolio, not a CV. BenchLab was designed for the economy that is arriving.

02

Physical skills endure

AI cannot pour a candle, compound a fragrance, or run a GMP-compliant production batch. The bench-level expertise BenchLab serves is the most durable in the economy — the skills that technology cannot replace.

03

Project-based futures

The shift from fixed employment to project-based working is already underway. BenchLab’s consultant marketplace is the infrastructure for skilled specialists to market their capabilities to multiple clients — the future of how expert work gets done.
